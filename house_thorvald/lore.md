# Lore — House Thorvald

Source: [research/House_Thorvald_Lore_Research.pdf](research/House_Thorvald_Lore_Research.pdf), a four-page research write-up the user
supplied on 2026-10-30. It draws on fan wikis (Dune Wiki on Fandom, the Dune: Awakening community wiki), the Modiphius RPG books and
the Dune: Imperium designer notes. It is a compilation, not a primary source, so where it gives a date or a cause of death, treat that as
"what the wikis say". Points marked **(ours)** are our own ideas built on top of it, not canon.

## The house in one paragraph
House Thorvald is a **House Major of the Landsraad** in the last decades of the Corrino Empire. Its homeworld is **Ipyr**, a world of
rolling temperate hills and long mountain ranges, but its wealth comes from space: it mines the **Ipyrian Asteroid Belt** and smelts
the ore in **Heighforge**, an orbital refinery so large that Ipyr's own people call it a moon. Heighforge makes exotic, high-durability
alloys that warships, commercial tenders and planetary defence platforms across the Imperium are built from. The house motto is
**"The hammer of wealth"**: money and force are one tool. Its nobles are trained at the **War Academy on Ipyr**, which teaches logistics,
supply chains and commercial theory next to battlefield command.

## What the lore gives the design

1. **Thorvald is anti-Corrino, but our faction is a Corrino clone.** The data clone (`Th_*`) still gives Thorvald Corrino's "Imperial army"
   flavour, Corrino's operations and Corrino's Sardaukar-style stats. The lore says the opposite: by 10191 Memnon is the Emperor's
   enemy. That is the biggest gap between story and data, and it is the main reason to give the house its own operations
   ([operations.md](operations.md)).
2. **Space first, ground second.** Heighforge is in orbit and the wealth is in the asteroid belt. That backs the air-heavy roster
   (Frigate, fighter, drone) and Guild Sub-Contract's airfield bonus ([faction_design.md](faction_design.md)), and it makes "a strike from
   Heighforge" the natural top-tier operation.
3. **"The hammer of wealth" is canon.** The 10000 Hegemony bonus name is the real motto, so keep it. It fits Iron Guard's
   Solari-not-Manpower rule: the house pays for its wars.
4. **The War Academy explains the councillors.** A school that teaches logistics and trade next to tactics produces exactly a logistics
   master (Silva), a market operator (Nobru) and a field commander (Jool).
5. **Alloys are the export.** Armour plate, hulls and vehicles are the house's pride. Units can be described as wearing and flying
   Heighforge alloy, and the heavy infantry can be the showcase for it.
6. **Industrial orange is the house trim.** Dune: Awakening's Thorvald swatch uses industrial orange trim, which settles the open
   question in [art_direction.md](art_direction.md) toward the hotter orange on the concept sheet rather than Sentinal's amber.

## Ideas built on the lore (ours)
- **Masks and wide hats as foundry gear.** The infantry's face masks and conical hats can be read as kit from the Heighforge smelting
  decks and the belt mines: masks against slag fumes and grit, wide brims against furnace glare and falling sparks. That gives the
  costume an in-world reason without making it the whole house's culture ([art_direction.md](art_direction.md)).
- **The tracked claw machine is a belt-mining rig on wheels.** A rock-saw and claw built for planetoids, put on tracks for Arrakis
  ([units.md](units.md)).
- **Firenza as a rallying cry.** One flavour line per faction screen or loading tip, e.g. "Ipyr remembers Firenza."
- **Mercenaries are an addition, not canon.** The canon house is a mining and alloy power. "The Mercenary Syndicate" on the faction
  screen is our layer: the house hires soldiers because it can afford to. Keep both, but lead with the industry.

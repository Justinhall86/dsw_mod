# Name candidates — House Thorvald

Started 2026-11-11. **Nothing here is decided or applied.** No cdb row, character, portrait or doc uses these names yet. The owner
has said a final name for the military councillor will come from him; these lists are raw material for that and for any new character.

## The brief

- The military councillor is Envoy Theodoric Ein in the cdb (`ThEin`), a working name that replaced Overseer Jool on 2026-11-02.
- The owner also floated a cousin or nephew for the family.
- Names should sound Norse, Germanic, Greek or Japanese-style. The house is already mixed: Memnon is Greek, Thorvald is Norse, Haruka is Japanese, Silva is Latin or Portuguese, Karlin is Germanic. The mix is not a rule; Memnon and Thorvald stay the anchors.
- Avoid stereotypes. Greek side: no Dimitri, Nikos, Stavros or Yiannis, no -opoulos or -akis endings, no god names (Zeus, Apollo, Adonis) as first names. Norse side: no Ragnar or Bjorn, few "-sson" endings. Japanese side: no famous clan names (Takeda, Oda, Tokugawa), no Hanzo or Musashi, no honorifics, no words like ronin or shogun.

## Origins of the existing names (checked in chat 2026-11-11)

| Name | Origin |
|---|---|
| Thorvald | Old Norse Þorvaldr, "Thor's ruler" (Scandinavian, not Spanish) |
| Memnon | Greek myth: king of the Aethiopians, killed at Troy |
| Theodoric | Germanic ("people-ruler"; Gothic and Old High German). The Old Norse form is Þjóðrekr. Dietrich is the German form. **Check the canon spelling in Dune: Awakening**: the owner wrote "theodric" |
| Ein | The German word for "one" (also Norwegian Nynorsk "ein"). The canon surname's origin is not confirmed |

# Identity and lore

## One sentence
House Ginaz is the House of the blade: a Sword-School aristocracy whose soldiers are duellists in long wine-red coats,
who reject shields and firearms on principle and win through evasion, parries and ritual combat.

## Symbols
- **Emblem:** two crossed rapiers above three wavy lines (water / sand dunes), gold on wine. Drawn most clearly on the banner image
  (`images/emblem_banner_crossed_rapiers.png`). The waves tie the House to Arrakis; the rapiers are the school.
- **Banner:** a wine pennant with a pointed end and a gold border. Used for the faction screens and as a background in unit art.
- **Sword-School crest (secondary):** one image shows a winged trident on a "GINAZ SCHOOL" banner. Treat it as a possible crest for the
  school's elite/hero units only, never as the House emblem.
- **Colours:** wine and plum, gold trim, dark-brown leather, black hair and beards. Full palette with hex values in
  [art_direction.md](art_direction.md).

## Tone
Formal, proud, ritualised. They treat combat as an art with rules: duels are challenged, honour is kept, a Kanly (blood feud)
is declared properly. Their discipline is the point: calm, precise, economical. Visually: officers and swordsmen, not
brawlers; a matador or naval captain more than a soldier.

## What the House is NOT
- Not a gunpowder/lasgun House: **no firearms**. Not a shield House: **no shields**. These are both the identity and the drawback.
- Not a heavy-armour House: armour is leather and cloth; status shows in gold embroidery, not steel plate.
- Not Sardaukar-styled: no Imperial grey/black plate or horned helms (also a standing rule for the models we make).
- Not a mystery/assassin cult in look (that is Bene Gesserit / Smuggler territory), although the House takes assassin contracts.

## Vocabulary (used in the concept art; check against Dune canon before shipping text)
| Term | Meaning in the concept |
|---|---|
| **Sword-School** | the institution that trains Ginaz duellists; "Sword-School Master" is a rank/aura source |
| **Grandmaster** | a named school master offered as the faction's leader/hero |
| **Kanly** | the formal blood feud between Houses; declaring one is a faction action; "Rival" = a House with an active Kanly |
| **Riposte / Mastery / Momentum** | stacking combat buffs earned by parrying, hitting or dodging (three names for the same idea in different drafts) |
| **Ritual Duel** | an active ability: challenge one enemy unit, gain power against it, disable its specials |
| **Champion** | one elite melee unit named by the player (5K hegemony), gaining health and an execute |
| **Authority / Influence / Hegemony / Solari / Manpower** | the game's own resources and scores |
| **Lasgun / Moritani / War of Assassins** | named in a Grandmaster blurb: lasguns are the weapon duellists must slip past; the Moritani and a "War of Assassins" are the House's remembered enemies (unverified lore) |

Dune background the design leans on (general canon, not from the images): the Swordmasters of Ginaz are the Imperium's famed blade
fighters (Duncan Idaho trained among them), blades remain meaningful because personal shields repel fast blows, and a Kanly is a
formally declared vendetta with rules. The images add House-specific names (Cassian Vos, Selene Zohar, Mei Ling, Corin Ginaz)
that are invented for this project.

## How the House plays (pillars)
1. **Duel, don't brawl:** small elite squads that win fights by not taking damage (evasion, parry) and punishing attackers.
2. **Stacks reward continuity:** every parry/hit/dodge feeds a stacking buff, so fights that go on favour Ginaz.
3. **Cheap to keep, costly to field badly:** supply-efficient infantry that live in the Deep Desert, but a small roster and no ranged/shield safety net.
4. **Honour as a resource:** Kanly, duels and Hegemony from melee kills turn violence into score.
5. **Leaders amplify:** auras from Heroes/Elites/Grandmasters multiply what the footmen already do.

## Weaknesses (needed for balance and for story)
- Cannot field firearms or shields at all: weak against massed ranged fire, artillery and anything that out-ranges rapiers.
- Few unit types, melee-only: vulnerable when pinned from range or kited.
- Evasion/parry are chance-based: variance, and low value against area damage.
- The whole faction identity needs melee contact, so mobility and engagement control matter more than raw armour.

## In-world framing from the faction screens
- By Imperial decree the swordmasters stand ready to claim **Stewardship of Arrakis**: the premise every Great House has in the game.
- They have "risen to become a storied Great House" (the image says "House Major"; the game says Great House) and come to Arrakis "to prove the supremacy of their martial school".
- "The most feared dueling force in the galaxy, bound by ancient honor and intense rivalries": the rivalries are the Kanly mechanic.
- Their leader is the Lord of the House (named Lord Corin Ginaz in the art; **the name is under review**, see [leaders_and_councilors.md](leaders_and_councilors.md)).

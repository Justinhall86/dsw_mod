# Councilor Concepts — New House (unnamed)

Draft councilor ideas for the new faction being built (separate from House Ginaz — see `house_ginaz/` for that faction's files;
nothing for this house belongs in that folder).

## Jakob
- **Role:** Spacing Guild liaison
- **Ability:** Friendly with the Spacing Guild — -20% Spacing Guild requirements

## Siora
- **Role:** Spy expert
- **Ability:** Auto-sends Landsraad Guards to factions with low standing

## Herbert
- **Role:** General
- **Abilities (power-up):**
  - Grants all soldiers handgun ability, plus extra health, armor, and attack speed
  - Speeds up recruitment speed of soldiers
- **Status:** The handgun/health/armor/attack-speed power-up may need help fixing/balancing (possibly split into fewer, cleaner stat
  bonuses, or gated to an Elite unit only — to decide).

## Nier
- **Role:** Diplomatic
- **Ability:** Puts a matter to a Landsraad-style vote. If the vote goes against Nier's house, Landsraad Guards appear at 3 of the
  voting factions' cities (a retaliation/enforcement effect tied to losing the vote).

## Open decisions
- House name not yet finalized (folder currently just `house/`).
- Portraits, looks, and lore blurbs are not written yet.
- Herbert's kit needs balancing — likely too many stacked bonuses on one ability.
- Nier's vote ability needs mechanical detail: who can be targeted, vote odds/weighting, how many Landsraad Guards spawn per city, and
  whether this is an Active (cast) or a periodic/passive trigger.
